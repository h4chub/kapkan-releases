# H4 account-menu refinement

Public version stays 1.0.0. Private build ID: ui-accounts-h4.

- Reduced the top-right trigger to a single line with a chevron, a subtle hover surface and visible keyboard focus.
- Replaced the rectangular popup with a rounded charcoal surface, fine edge and soft composited shadow.
- Separated the current account from the Switch account list and Manage accounts action; shortened repeated profile-type labels.
- Kept pointer-open rows neutral until hovered, while keyboard-open menus retain a visible selection.
- Bounded the complete popup, including its shadow, inside the frame where possible; retained a native-popup fallback and restored cached-window styling after dismissal.
- Retained real account persistence, launch busy state, the six-profile limit, original images, official logo and every non-menu H3 visual decision.
- Rebuilt private Mac/Windows packages with H4 markers. Native-device and gameplay acceptance remain pending.
